# Showing The Pit in 10 minutes

You need: a laptop (or a TV) and the other person's phone. No setup on their side.

| Time | You do | They see |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open the link on the laptop. Point at the three lines under the logo. | What it is: write the news, six Claude traders argue and trade, you trade against them, every call is scored. |
| 0:30 | Tap **Run the demo**, then **Stage** (top right). Read out the room code at the bottom, or tap **Room · invite** and text them the link. | The live market: candles, the order book, six trader cards with P&L. |
| 1:00 | They open the link on their phone and type a name. | Their phone becomes a trading terminal: price, buy/sell bar at the bottom. |
| 1:15 | **ACT II · BREAKING NEWS**: "CFO resigns…" hits the wire. | NEWS → the desk's read (fair value −x%) → **FLOOR DEBATE**: six opening views, challenges by name, someone changes their mind. |
| 1:45 | **ACT IV · YOUR MOVE.** Tell them: buy the panic or join it, now. | Their order prints on the tape and on the chart in their colour. The **Timeline** tab (Storyline) shows every step in order. |
| 2:15 | **ACT V–VII**: dealers pull out, a fund dumps, **circuit breaker**. | The regime flips to PANIC / LIQUIDITY CRUNCH, the book empties, the halt banner, then the reopen. |
| 2:45 | **ACT VIII · THE TWIST**: interim CFO, no wrongdoing. | Does the floor reverse? Watch who flips. |
| 3:15 | **ACT IX · THE SCORES.** | Right / wrong for every trader's call on the CFO news, 60 s later, from the real price. |
| 3:45 | The bell rings itself: the **Daily wrap**. | Standings, Humans vs AI, best and worst call, fastest reaction, new records, the AI science verdict, Claude's match report. |
| 5:00 | Close the wrap. Tap a trader card (try Pip). | Playbook, claimed edge vs measured best category, calibration ("90% sure, right 40% of the time"), lessons they wrote themselves. |
| 6:00 | Tap the **AI SCIENCE** strip. | "Debate improved / hurt the floor: opening views x% → final y%", herding in panic vs calm, measured rivals and twins. |
| 7:00 | **Host · lab → Start a new session** (or the button on the wrap). | **Previously on The Pit** with the last session's titled moments and records. |
| 7:30 | Hand them the controls: **Host · lab → Chaos controls → Short squeeze**, then **AI panic**. | The engine reacts first (forced buying), then the AI floor reads the risk-desk memo and decides for itself. |
| 9:00 | They write their own headline in the newsroom ("CEO arrested at airport"). | The floor reacts to *their* news. That's the moment it clicks. |
| 9:45 | Ring the **closing bell**. Open the lobby. | **Legends**: the records you just set, kept on your server. |

The demo uses a fixed seed (1987), so the crowd, the dealers and the offline fallback
play out the same every time; Claude's words differ run to run by design. It works
with no keys at all (mock traders), but it's at its best with the Anthropic key set.
